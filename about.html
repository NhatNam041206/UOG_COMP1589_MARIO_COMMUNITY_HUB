About Us

Mario Community Hub brings fan culture, karting fun, and friendly regional events together in one welcoming space.

Friends & Fans

From local meetups to a regional hub

Mario Community Hub started in Vietnam as a small fan circle built by friends who loved two things: Super Mario and local karting nights. Our first meetups were simple, social, and full of energy around Ho Chi Minh City and Hanoi.

As the community grew, we moved from casual hangouts to better-planned events. We kept the friendly vibe, but added clearer race-day guidance, safer activity setup, and smoother scheduling for larger groups.

By 2026, the hub had grown into a regional community across Southeast Asia. Today we focus on three things: fun karting experiences, memorable fan events, and premium collectibles that celebrate every big moment.

Our goal stays simple: make every meetup feel easy to join, safe to enjoy, and worth coming back to with your friends.

How We Grew (2024-2026)

Period Milestone
Early 2024 We hosted our first unofficial "Mushroom Cup" meetup at a local Ho Chi Minh City track and saw strong early interest in friendly racing.
Mid 2024 We started working with local track partners so events could run with clearer safety checks and smoother day-of coordination.
Late 2024 The "Member Gilded Coin" pilot launched and introduced limited collectible coins as a new community keepsake.
Early 2025 Membership reached 5,000 active members across Vietnam and Singapore, supported by tiered programs such as the "Pit Crew" level.
Mid 2025 We hosted the first "Peach's Garden" fan convention with social activities, networking corners, and creator meetups.
Late 2025 Beginner karting sessions were refreshed with simple coaching tips so first-time racers could feel confident on track.
February 2026 We launched the Mario Hub digital portal to keep registration, scheduling, and event check-ins in one place.
April 2026 The community expanded to more fan meetups across Asia-Pacific, helping us support larger regional events while keeping the same welcoming community spirit.
